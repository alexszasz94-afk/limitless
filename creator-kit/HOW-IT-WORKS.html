Claude
  • Next unused woman and room from the library (13 women, 26 rooms included). When they run low, it finds new ones on Pinterest by itself.

  • A new outfit under the robe each video, always long pants.
5

Claude makes the clips

Claude
  • Hook: their own shot, rebuilt with Kling 4.0 with our people and our robe. Same motion, same seconds, same spoken words. Their logos stay. Their caption text is removed with a clean photo (never blurred).
  • B-roll: our classic, tested motion clip: robe on, foot pockets, cozy. Feet always go into the pockets from outside.

Claude
  • Hook: side-by-side with the original, 10 frames a second. Same action in the same frame, voice on the same second.
  • B-roll: shot count, glitch detector and one sheet per shot. Person in every frame, same face, five fingers, robe and pockets like the photos.
  • Anything wrong is redone with the problem written into the prompt (up to 3 tries). No shot is ever dropped.

Claude

Learned the hard way

The rules that keep it right

Each one exists because something went wrong once. Claude applies all of them on every video.

Hook

  • Same motion, same speed, same seconds as the original. The robe falls as slowly as theirs.
  • Same words on the same seconds, voiced inside the clip.
  • Always different people from the original. It's a remake, not a copy.
  • Their logos and signs stay. Their caption is removed with a clean photo, never with blur.
  • The person is always in front, never behind a table or a plant.

B-roll

  • Only our classic, tested motion clip.
  • The person is in every shot. No empty robe, no missing head.
  • Both feet start outside, then go into the pockets one by one.
  • Every shot is essential. A bad shot is redone, never cut.

Look

  • Kling 4.0 at 1080p, images Nano Banana Pro at 4K.
  • Everyday iPhone look: bright, very soft natural light, true colours.
  • The robe is always copied from its photos, never described from memory.

Edit

  • Hook → robe on → pockets → connector → remote → cozy.
  • The reference's original text, word for word, never over a face.
  • Cuts land 0.12 s away from Kling's own cuts, where glitches hide.
