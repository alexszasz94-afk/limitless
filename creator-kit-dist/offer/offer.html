How it works

From a reel link to a finished video

  1. Your creator sends a reel and names the productOr describes an idea of their own. Plain English, no commands.
  2. The system studies the originalEvery action and every spoken word is mapped to the second, so the remake moves and sounds the same.
  3. New person, new room, your productFaces and rooms rotate automatically; new ones are sourced when the library runs low.
  4. Clips are generated and checkedHook, try-on, product features and the ending. Anything with a glitch is redone with the fix written in.
  5. Edited in a proven orderThe original's on-screen text, music with the chorus on the reveal, 1080×1920.
  6. Your creator postsThey get the MP4 and a caption. Their job is ideas and posting, nothing technical.
Your team's only job is creativity: picking reels that work, inventing new hooks, choosing the ending. Everything technical is handled by the system.
