# TREE PRIX — F1 Christmas Tree Racer — product spec for the supplier

Product name: **TREE PRIX**. Slogan on the box: "Lights out, Ho-Ho-Go!"
Design = the approved reference pack (`pine-prix-referinta/`), name changed to TREE PRIX.

Concept: like the classic train around the Christmas tree, but a miniature Formula 1 circuit shaped as a ring, mounted **on the tree** at mid-height. 4 F1 cars run one behind the other, all in the same direction, round and round the tree.

> Liveries and sponsor boards follow the reference pack (real teams/sponsors). This is Szasz's explicit choice (23 Oct). For real sales these are registered trademarks and need a licence; the supplier may require generic liveries.

## 1. How it mounts (from the "HOW IT MOUNTS" diagram)
1. **Trunk clamp** on the central pole of an artificial tree. Fits poles of **2–10 cm**, tightened with a knob. Tool-free.
2. **4 telescopic arms** go out from the clamp (black, adjustable length) and hold the ring level. The ring hangs in the branches; it does not stand on legs and does not touch the floor.
3. **8 track segments** clip onto the arms and form a **ring of about 70 cm** diameter.
4. The ring passes through the branches; the branch tips hide the arms.
5. **Power: USB-C**, cable runs down the trunk and is hidden in the branches.

## 2. How the cars move (they must never fly off)
- Under the track surface runs a **hidden drive ring** turned by **one small motor** in the clamp/hub.
- Each car **clips onto the drive ring** through a slot in the track. The cars are pulled round by the ring, so they cannot leave the track, overtake or collide; the 4 cars keep their spacing, one behind the other.
- Visually the cars ride on **two silver rails** (wheels on the rails, like a train).
- The same direction always; never diagonal, never head-on.

## 3. Track look (exactly as in the reference pack)
Dark grey asphalt, red-and-white kerbs, inner wall with sponsor boards, light-blue band on the edge, low silver armco fence, two silver rails.

## 4. Electronics
- **Remote:** on / off, speed, lights, sound.
- **Start lights** gantry (5 red lights: on 1 → 5, then lights out and the cars go) + **lap counter**.
- Sound module with real F1 engine sound.
- Low voltage only (USB-C, 5 V). Age 8+ with adult setup.

## 5. In the box
Top tray: the 4 cars (1:43). Bottom tray: 8 track segments, sponsor walls/fences, trunk clamp, start-lights bar, 4 telescopic arms, remote, USB-C cable. Instructions.

## 6. Cars
Scale 1:43 (~ 13 cm long). Red Bull #3 (navy), Ferrari #44 (red), McLaren #1 (papaya), Mercedes #63 (silver). ABS body, clip under the chassis for the drive ring.

## 7. Box
Navy matte, red-and-white stripe on the edges, gold **TREE PRIX** logo (small tree + checkered flag between the words), gold slogan "Lights out, Ho-Ho-Go!".
