Robe Studio · the complete guide

One reel in, a finished robe video out.

Robe Studio makes ready-to-post TikTok and Reels videos for our heated hooded robes. You send it a reel that is working for someone else, or an idea of your own. It rebuilds the video with our robe, a new person and a new room, checks every frame, edits it with text and music, and hands you the MP4.

You never edit anything. No video editor, no files, no code, no settings. You talk to Claude in normal sentences, like texting a friend who edits videos for you. If you want something different, you just say it.

Deep dive · hooks

How a reel is copied

When you send a link, Claude doesn't just "get inspired". It rebuilds the reel's hook so it moves and sounds like the original, but with our robe and different people.

  1. Downloads the reel and studies it frame by frame.It writes down every action with its second and speed, for example "0–2.7 s she sways the rolled robe slowly; at 2.8 s she lets it unroll".
  2. Removes their caption, cleanly.The text they put on top of their video is removed using a clean photo of the same spot, never with blur (blur would show up as a smudge in our video).
  3. Keeps their place as it is.Store signs and logos in the scene stay exactly as in the original. Only the people and the product change.
  4. Changes the people.Our person replaces theirs, and anyone else in the shot gets a clearly different face, so it is a remake, never a copy.
  5. Generates the new hook from their real footage.The motion, speed and seconds stay identical: if their robe falls slowly at 2.8 s, ours does too.
  6. Compares ours with theirs, side by side.Ten pictures per second, one row each. Anything faster, slower or different is regenerated.
On-screen text: the video uses the reference's original text, word for word, with the same emojis. It worked for them; we keep it.

Your own hooks

When you describe an idea, Claude creates a realistic photo of the first moment with our person in her room, and shows it to you. You say OK or what to change, then how it should move and whether someone says something. Claude animates it, checks it and builds the rest of the video around it.

Deep dive · sound

Voices and music

When people talk in the original

If the reel has a voice ("Three, two, one… let's see how it turns out!"), our hook says the same words on the same seconds. This step is never skipped:

  1. Who says what.Higgsfield's video analysis listens to the reel and writes down who speaks (man or woman, which tone) and the exact words.
  2. When each word falls.A speech tool marks the second of every word, for example "three" at 0.0 s, "two" at 0.8 s, "one" at 1.9 s.
  3. The voice is made inside the clip.The words, the voice and the timing go into the video generation itself, so the lips move with the words.
  4. Checked afterwards.Claude compares the words and seconds of our hook with the original. If they don't match, the hook is made again.

Music

  • Each robe has its own songs (for example Linus and Lucy for Snoopy, Let It Go for Princess). The chorus or the beat drop is placed exactly on the reveal.
  • Want a different song? Drag the MP3 into the chat and say "use this song". Claude finds the best part and places it.
  • When the hook has a voice, the music goes quieter so the words are clear.
Deep dive · faces and places

People and rooms

Every video uses a new person and a new room, so your account never shows the same girl twice in a row.

Where they come from

  • The kit comes with 13 women and 26 rooms, real photos of American-style cozy Christmas homes.
  • When fewer than 5 are left, Claude searches Pinterest by itself, looks at every photo and keeps only those that pass: one woman, clear face not covered by a cup or phone, eyes open, real-looking, good light; rooms that are bright, real, with a Christmas tree, floor space and a sofa or a bed.
  • Bad photos are thrown out automatically. You can also just ask for new ones anytime.

How the room is used

  • Room check: sofa → the cozy shot happens on the sofa; only a bed → on the bed; neither → the room is skipped.
  • Always in the foreground: the person is never behind a table, a plant or a chair. If the room photo has a table in the middle, Claude first makes a version of the room without it.
  • One place per video: the same room for the hook (when it's at home), the robe, the connector, the remote and the cozy ending.
  • Outfit under the robe: a different one each video (Christmas pajamas, a cozy set, a college sweatshirt), always long pants.
Deep dive · showing the robe

The product part

  • B-roll: made from our classic, tested movement clip, rebuilt with the new person, the new room and the robe copied exactly from its photos. The robe is never "described"; it is always reproduced from the real product pictures.
  • Foot pockets: both feet start outside on the floor, then go in one by one. Never a foot already inside.
  • Every shot counts: no shot is ever dropped to hide a problem. A bad shot is redone.
  • Connector and remote: our real close-ups, with the background changed to that video's room.
  • Cozy ending: the classic one from the B-roll, or a new 5-second shot of whatever you ask for: reading on the bed, hot cocoa on the sofa, wrapping gifts by the tree, a movie under the hood.
Quality

How mistakes are caught

AI video sometimes makes mistakes: a hand with six fingers, a robe that melts, a person who disappears. Robe Studio looks for them before you ever see the video.

CheckWhat it catches
Every photo, looked atWrong face, wrong robe, extra fingers, text where there shouldn't be any
Hook vs originalMovement too fast or too slow, actions at the wrong second
Voice vs originalMissing words, words at the wrong time, a silent hook
B-roll shot countA missing or invented shot
Glitch detectorSudden jumps inside a shot: melting, a photo flashing in, a body part popping
Shot-by-shot lookEmpty robe, missing head, second robe, wrong pockets, blur patches
Safe cutsCuts are placed away from the spots where AI glitches hide
Final videoText over a face, black frames, music, order of the parts

Anything that fails is redone with the problem written into the instructions, up to 3 times. If something still isn't perfect, Claude tells you exactly what and why.

Your account stays fresh

Staying fresh

Robe Studio remembers what each video used: hook, person, room, outfit, music, on-screen text and cozy ending. The next video is always different from the last three, so followers never feel they're watching the same thing again. Before each video Claude also suggests a cozy ending you haven't used lately.

Honest limits

How far it can go

Works great

  • Reels where one or two people do something with an object: reveals, countdowns, unboxings, try-ons, store displays, reactions.
  • Hooks with spoken lines, matched on the second.
  • Your own ideas: any scene you can describe in a sentence or two.
  • Many videos a day: each takes about 20 minutes.

Harder

  • Crowds or choreography with many people moving at once.
  • Very fast cuts every half second.
  • Tiny details close up, like text on a phone screen.
  • Claude can't post for you or promise a video goes viral. The ideas you pick make the difference.

New robes, new formats and improvements arrive through Alex's updates, about once a week.

Where you make the difference

Your part

The technical work is handled. Your energy goes into ideas:

  • Find reels that already work: short (10–20 s), lots of views, a strong first 3 seconds.
  • Invent your own hooks: try one of your own for every two or three copied reels.
  • Change the cozy ending often.
  • Post consistently, and notice which hooks get the most views. Do more of those.
Help

If something goes wrong

  • Claude says Higgsfield isn't connected: open claude.ai/customize/connectors, connect Higgsfield again, start a new chat.
  • Claude says a download was blocked: check the Network access step in the setup guide, then start a new chat.
  • Credits ran out: top up on higgsfield.ai.
  • Anything else: ask what went wrong? and Claude explains and fixes it. Still stuck? Write to Alex.
