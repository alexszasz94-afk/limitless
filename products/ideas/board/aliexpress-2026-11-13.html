Product research · AliExpress · 13 Nov 2026

10 products to test this Christmas

Picked for emotion in the first three seconds and for scenes we can exaggerate without lying about the product. Every product is a hypothesis until our own numbers come in from a pilot video.

  • 1. Magic Trail Wall LightThe only one with a wave of fresh listings and big order counts already (2,000+ and 1,000+).
  • 2. Sleigh Pet BedHighest emotion score (44/50) and a fresh wave of listings, but few orders so far.
  • 3. Felt Tree Cat HouseNot new, but the idea has demand proof: a related product reportedly hit 34M views on one video.

Not verified yet: shipping cost and delivery days to the US (product pages need a login), how many accounts already run each product on Instagram and TikTok, and dollars per 1M views. Prices, orders and ratings were read from AliExpress search pages on 13 Nov 2026. "New" is an estimate from the listing number and from how many fresh listings appeared together. Christmas items must reach buyers before Dec 25, so the selling window closes in early December.
