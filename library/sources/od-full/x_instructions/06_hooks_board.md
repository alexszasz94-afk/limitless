# 06 — Hooks Board

**What it is:** `x_systems/hooks/hooks_board.html` (open in Chrome or Edge) holds the **14 proven
hooks**: the C01 opening shots that made videos take off. They are grouped into 4 concepts
(Store discovery, The 3-2-1 reveal, Gifting POV, Pattern interrupt). Each hook shows its
clip, its starting frame and its reference, plus where it came from (product, video, shot)
and what changed between versions. A card at the top says what is true across all 14.

**Text on screen:** a card listing the on-screen hook captions that worked (for example
"Do NOT let a Christmas mom see this 🎄🎁"). The list lives in
`x_systems/hooks/text_on_screen.txt`. In Chrome / Edge, click **Connect text_on_screen.txt
(once)** and pick that file; after that every edit on the board saves into it automatically.

**Files:** `hooks_board.html` (a hand-maintained page, not generated by the board kit),
`assets/hook_NN/` (the hook's frame `.png`, clip `.mp4`, references, named like the product
files), `thumbs/` (the board's thumbnails), `text_on_screen.txt`.

**How to use it:** when planning a new video, start from a hook here. To remake a hook for
another product, open the source shot's `prompts.md` (the board names product, video and
shot) and follow CLAUDE.md rule 3: copy the prompt verbatim and change only the nouns.

This board is meant to grow: future systems were to be added to it. Don't restructure it
unless your user asks.
