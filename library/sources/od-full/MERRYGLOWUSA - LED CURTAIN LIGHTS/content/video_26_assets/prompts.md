### What made it work

“Space at the top” means a thin strip, not a band of sky. Eight passes bounced between “too little space at the top” and “too much space and the camera it’s too far away”, because the model treats headroom and camera distance as the same dial. They have to be given separately and in the same sentence: keep the subject this size, add this much empty sky.

Once the angle is right, freeze it in words. g9 opens with “THE ANGLE IS CORRECT AND MUST NOT CHANGE” and then describes it geometrically — the roof edge enters LOW at the LEFT edge and exits HIGH at the RIGHT edge. Edge-by-edge geometry survives a chained edit; “more oblique” and “like @Image 1” do not.
