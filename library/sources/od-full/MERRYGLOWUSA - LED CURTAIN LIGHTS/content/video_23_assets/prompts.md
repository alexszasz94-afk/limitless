1. **For "same framing as X", attach X and write a SHORT prompt that names only what changes.** Long geometric re-descriptions compete with the attached reference and win — that's what caused the v22 C2 drift across four takes. Every C2–C7 prompt here is under 200 words.

3. **A short prompt is not enough on its own — the SHORT prompt has to be attached to the RIGHT reference.** C2 g1 was short and still broke the sofa, because the only image attached was the v22 frame and the prompt spent all three of its points on *changes* (camera, woman, room) without ever restating the sofa geometry.
