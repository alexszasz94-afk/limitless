# F1-style Christmas Tree Racer — product spec for the supplier

Concept: like the classic train that runs under the Christmas tree, but a modern open-wheel (F1-style) race car that runs on a track **mounted on the tree itself**, climbing up and coming back down around it in an endless loop.

No licensed teams, no logos, no sponsor decals, no numbers. Liveries in the colours of current F1 cars (red/black, papaya/black, navy/red-yellow, silver/teal).

## 1. How the car stays on the track (it must never fly off)
It is a **slot-car system** (the same principle as Scalextric / Carrera), plus a magnet:

1. **Guide slot + guide blade.** The track has a narrow slot running down the middle. Under the car's nose there is a pivoting guide blade (a thin vertical fin, about 8 mm deep) that sits inside the slot. The car is physically locked to the line of the track and steers itself through every curve.
2. **Magnet traction.** A neodymium magnet under the chassis is pulled down onto the steel power rails of the track. That keeps the car pressed to the track on climbs, on descents and in banked curves, so it does not tip over or jump out of the slot.
3. **Banked curves + guard wall.** All curves are banked inward by about 5°. Every curve has a low clear guard wall on its outer edge (about 15 mm high) as a last safety barrier.
4. **Speed limiter.** The power box caps the maximum voltage so that the top speed is safe for the tightest radius and the steepest grade. There is no "turbo" that can throw the car out.

## 2. Power
- Two thin steel/copper rails run on each side of the slot and carry low-voltage DC (12 V max) from a wall adapter to the power box.
- Two small copper braids (contact brushes) on each side of the guide blade pick up the current. A small DC motor drives the rear axle.
- **Handheld controller:** a trigger speed controller with a cable, or optionally wireless 2.4 GHz.
- **Auto mode:** a switch on the power box makes the car run on its own at a constant slow "display" speed, like the Christmas train.
- **LED lights:** small LEDs in the car (front-wing light strip and a red rear rain light), powered from the rails.
- Low voltage only. Child-safe: 8+ with adult setup.

## 3. Track layout on the tree (endless loop)
- **Double helix**, like a parking-garage ramp. One spiral climbs (about 3 turns), the second spiral is interleaved between the turns of the first and comes back down. They are joined by a hairpin at the top and a hairpin at the bottom, so the loop is closed and endless.
- **Maximum grade: 8%.** For example, base radius about 45–55 cm, circumference about 3 m per turn, rise about 22–25 cm per turn.
- Radius shrinks towards the top to follow the cone of the tree. Supply 3 radius sizes (L / M / S) of curve pieces.
- Single lane, about 60 mm wide. A 2-lane racing version (2 cars, 2 controllers) is a possible upgrade.
- Pieces snap together with tabs. Rails connect through metal joiners.

## 4. Mounting (the tree carries almost no weight)
- **Telescopic centre pole** (3 sections, black, steel or aluminium, up to about 180 cm). It stands on a weighted base ring that sits around the tree stand, and is zip-tied or clamped to the trunk.
- **Support arms** (8 per level, black, adjustable length) go out radially from collar clamps on the pole. Each arm ends in a track holder that clips under the track.
- **Clear branch clips** only stabilise the track against the branches. They do not carry the weight.
- Fits artificial and real trees from 6 to 7.5 ft. Assembly is tool-free.

## 5. In the box
1 car (more cars and colours sold separately) · track pieces for the full double helix plus the 2 hairpins · telescopic pole + base ring · support arms + collars · branch clips · power box with speed limiter and auto mode · handheld controller · wall adapter · spare guide blade + spare brushes · instructions.

## 6. Car
- Scale 1:32 (about 15–17 cm long). Modern F1 shape: halo, multi-element front wing, big rear wing, slick tyres.
- Body: ABS plastic. Chassis: plastic with the magnet pocket, motor pod and guide-blade mount.
- Liveries without any logos or text: red/black, papaya/black, navy/red-yellow, silver/teal.

- Can they make a double-helix track with an 8% max grade, or do they already have a climbing slot track?
- How much does the full set weigh?
