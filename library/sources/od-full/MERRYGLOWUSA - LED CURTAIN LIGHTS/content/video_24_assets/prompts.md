### What made it work

Two references need two explicit, single jobs — stated before any description. “@Image 1 governs FRAMING, CAMERA and POSE ONLY” and “@Image 2 governs WHO THE WOMAN IS ONLY” are the first two lines of the prompt. Without that split the model blends the two rooms and you get a third room that is neither.

The prompt names them one by one — heart, comment and share icons, play button, speaker icon, caption, username, profile picture, status bar, comment bar — and closes with “no text of any kind anywhere in the picture.”
