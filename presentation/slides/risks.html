Risks

What can go wrong, and our answer

Christmas window

Sales stop after Dec 20. → Launch by mid-November, US stock for late orders

Heating safety

Uncertified parts mean refunds, chargebacks. → Only suppliers with safety certificates and good ratings, written test reports

Payment holds

Processor keeps ~20% of cash. → Keep a cash buffer, ship fast, answer every ticket

Account bans

Platforms limit new accounts. → Warm-up rules, real phones, never repost the same video

AI detection and IP

→ Natural UGC look, no brands, no licensed prints, follow labeling rules

Duty and costs

Tariffs could rise to 37.5%. → Margin still positive; price set with room

All numbers here are estimates until the first product is tested with real orders.
